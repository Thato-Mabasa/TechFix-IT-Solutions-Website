About Us

Built by technicians, for small business owners

We started TechFix because too many small businesses were choosing between overpriced enterprise IT contracts and no support at all.

Our History

From one laptop repair to a full support desk

TechFix IT Solutions was founded in 2019 in Johannesburg by a small team of technicians who were tired of watching local businesses struggle with IT problems they didn't have the time or budget to solve. What began as informal desktop repairs for neighbouring shops has grown into a dedicated IT support practice serving over 120 small and medium businesses across Gauteng — from retail stores and accounting firms to non-profits and medical practices.

We've kept the same philosophy since day one: IT support should be fast, honest, and priced for a small business budget, not an enterprise one.

Mission

Keep small businesses running

To give small and medium businesses dependable, jargon-free IT support so they can focus on running their business, not fixing their network.

Vision

IT support without the enterprise price tag

To become Gauteng's most trusted IT partner for small businesses, proving that responsive, quality support doesn't require an in-house IT department.

Who We Serve

Our target audience

TechFix works primarily with small and medium businesses (5–50 employees) in Johannesburg and the wider Gauteng region who don't have — or don't need — a full-time, in-house IT team. Typical clients include retail stores, accounting and legal practices, medical rooms, and local non-profits that rely on a small number of computers and a stable network to operate day to day.
